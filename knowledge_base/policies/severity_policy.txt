ONCALL SEVERITY POLICY (ShopNest Platform Team)

P1 - CRITICAL. Definition: a production outage, data loss or security breach affecting all or most users, or a core
business flow (checkout, payment, login) failing. Examples: primary database down, public TLS certificate expired,
502 on all upstreams, disk full on a database host. Response: page the on-call engineer immediately, acknowledge
within 5 minutes, open an incident channel and a status page entry, update stakeholders every 30 minutes.
Postmortem required within 5 business days.

P2 - MAJOR. Definition: production degraded for a significant subset of users or a non-core flow failing; the system
works with retries or at reduced capacity. Examples: error rate 5-50% on some replicas, OOM restarts on one service,
p99 latency far above SLO, a deployment rolled back after partial failure. Response: respond within 30 minutes during
on-call hours; status page entry if customer-visible; postmortem recommended.

P3 - MINOR. Definition: limited impact, a single host or replica, an issue in staging that blocks testing, intermittent
errors with successful retries, or an issue that self-recovered. Response: handle during working hours; create a
ticket; no page outside working hours.

P4 - LOW. Definition: informational events, single occurrences in dev, rates within the normal baseline, events already
auto-resolved with no user impact. Response: log for the record; review in the weekly operations meeting.

ESCALATION RULES. Any incident on a customer-facing production path with revenue impact is at least P2. Security
incidents (successful unauthorised access, credential leak) are P1 regardless of user impact and must be escalated to
the security team. If the on-call engineer cannot restore service within 30 minutes for a P1, escalate to the service
owner and the engineering manager.

COMMUNICATION. Incident channel naming: #inc-<date>-<service>. Status updates: every 30 minutes for P1, hourly for P2.
Never share secrets, tokens or customer personal data in the incident channel or in the incident report.
