INCIDENT REPORT TEMPLATE

Title: <service>: <short description>
Severity: P1 | P2 | P3 | P4
Status: Investigating | Identified | Monitoring | Resolved
Detected at: <timestamp>   Reported by: <alert or person>
Affected services and environment: <list>
User impact: <who was affected, how, for how long>

Summary: Two or three sentences describing what happened in plain language.

Timeline: chronological list of detection, key findings, mitigation steps and recovery, each with a timestamp.

Root cause: the underlying technical cause, distinguished from the trigger and from contributing factors.

Mitigation applied: what was done to restore service.

Permanent fix / action items: owner, action, due date for each.

Lessons learned: what to detect earlier next time, what to automate, what documentation to improve.
